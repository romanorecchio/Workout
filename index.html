💡 How this works

Weights are calculated from your 115lb bench. Tap a lift to see exact weights + warm-ups. Check off each set. Hit the top rep on all sets → I'll tell you to add weight.

📐 Double Progression Rule

Start at the LOW end of the rep range. Add 1 rep per set each session. Hit the TOP rep on ALL sets → add 5 lbs next session. Repeat forever.
